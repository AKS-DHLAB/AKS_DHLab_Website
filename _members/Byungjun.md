## **RESEARCH PROFILE**

My research lies in digital humanities and computational social science: I analyze large-scale bibliographic and textual data to study how scholarly and cultural knowledge is produced and communicated. (디지털인문학과 전산사회과학을 중심으로, 대규모 서지·텍스트 데이터를 분석해 학술 지식과 문화 지식이 생산되고 소통되는 방식을 연구합니다.)

**Keywords**: digital humanities · computational social science · cultural analytics · bibliometrics and science of science · natural language processing and text mining · scholarly communication and open access · Korean datasets and language models
